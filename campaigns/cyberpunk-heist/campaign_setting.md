# CAMPAIGN SETTING: THE LIMINAL ZONE

## THEME
"It's Always Sunny in Cyberpunk" meets "Alphas". A darkly comedic, highly dysfunctional survival story about maintaining a perfect facade in a dystopian corporate utopia.

## LOCATION: THE PERIMETER
The sanitized, corporate suburbs right on the edge of the Mega-Corp Utopia. It is clean on the surface, but the neon glow of the lower slums leaks through the cracks. The characters live here, clinging to their "passable" corporate lives. They are constantly one missed payment, one bad performance review, or one slipped secret away from being deported down into the grimy neon undercity. 

## THE DYNAMIC (IMPOSTER SYNDROME)
In public, the characters are desperately trying to act like sleek, perfect, utopian citizens. Behind closed doors, they are chaotic, panicking, and pulling illicit gigs to maintain the facade, hiding their bizarre fringe-science quirks from the Mega-Corp.
